Privacy Policy

BoothNet · Booth Company Real Estate, LLC · Effective October 11, 2026

BoothNet is a private business-management tool used internally by Adam Booth of Booth Company Real Estate, LLC. It is not offered to the public, and no one outside Booth Company Real Estate signs in to it.

What BoothNet accesses

With the account owner's permission, granted through Google's own sign-in screen, BoothNet accesses:

  • Google Calendar: to show the owner's appointments for the day.
  • Gmail (read): to recognize incoming lead notifications (for example, website contact-form and real-estate portal emails) and add those leads to the owner's contact list.
  • Gmail (drafts): to prepare email drafts that the owner reviews and sends himself. BoothNet does not send email on its own.

How the information is used and stored

  • Information is used only to run Booth Company Real Estate's own client follow-up and scheduling.
  • Data is stored locally on the owner's computer. It is not uploaded to third-party servers by BoothNet.
  • Information is never sold, rented, or shared for advertising.
  • BoothNet's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
